SECTION 2: DEDUCTIBLES AND CO-PAYMENTS The Insured is responsible for a $2,500 annual deductible for structural damage and a $2,500 annual deductible for flood damage (if endorsed). SECTION 3: LOSS OF USE If the residence becomes uninhabitable due to a covered peril, the Company will cover temporary living expenses up to 15% of the dwelling limit. Temporary housing coverage is strictly limited to 14 days of accommodation. SECTION 4: CLAIMS TIMELINE All claims under this standard policy must be filed within 45 days of the incident. Claimant must submit Form 10-H to the claims department. SECTION: DEFINITIONS For the purposes of this Policy, the following definitions shall apply unless the context clearly indicates otherwise. 1. "Effective Date" indicates the exact date and time when the coverage under this Policy commences. 6. "Expiration Date" designates the exact date and time when the coverage under this Policy terminates, subject to renewal conditions. 7. "Bodily Injury" implies physical harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including death resulting from any of these at any time. 8. "Property Damage" signifies physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. 9. "Occurrence" refers to an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in Bodily Injury or Property Damage during the policy period. 10. "Claim" means a formal demand by the Insured or a third party for compensation under the terms of this Policy. 11. "Deductible" is the initial amount of a covered loss that the Insured is responsible for paying before the Company begins to pay for the covered loss. 12. "Endorsement" means a written amendment attached to this Policy that modifies, adds, or restricts the terms of coverage. 13. "Exclusion" refers to specific situations, conditions, or circumstances that are not covered under this Policy. 14. "Limit of Liability" is the maximum amount the Company will pay for a covered loss, regardless of the actual magnitude of the loss. 15.
